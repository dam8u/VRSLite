## T9-assumptions. Assumption register

| ID | Assumption |
|---|---|
| ASM-01 | Mortality interpolation. SRC-20/SRC-21 publish rates at quinquennial ages only. Rates at intervening ages are interpolated log-linearly in q_x. Direction of error: negligible (<0.1% on annuity factors). |
| ASM-02 | Mortality extrapolation above age 90. The log-linear slope of q_x between ages 85 and 90 is continued, with q_x capped at 0.60 and a terminal age of 115. Pub-2010 actually decelerates at the oldest ages, so this slightly UNDERSTATES annuity values and therefore UNDERSTATES the measured subsidy. Effect on an annuity commencing at age 50-66: under 0.5%. |
| ASM-03 | Unisex blend. SRC-20 publishes sex-distinct rates. Representative profiles are valued on a 50/50 male/female blend of annuity factors unless a sex is specified. Sex-distinct results are reported alongside. |
| ASM-04 | Annuity form. The service retirement allowance is valued as a single-life annuity payable monthly, approximated as an annuity-due with a half-year timing adjustment. VRS's valuation assumes a modified cash refund annuity (return of any unrecovered member contributions). The modified cash refund floor is worth slightly more under the ACCELERATED case (a younger retiree with a smaller allowance recovers contributions more slowly), so ignoring it slightly UNDERSTATES the measured subsidy. |
| ASM-05 | Generational mortality improvement. VRS projects generationally with 75% of the MP-2021 scale (SRC-20). The MP-2021 rates are not reproduced in the published appendix. The central case applies a flat 0.75% per annum improvement to q_x from a 2010 base year to the year in which each age is attained, as a proxy. Sensitivity: 0.00% (no improvement) and 1.25%. Omitting improvement entirely understates annuity values by roughly 4-6%. |
| ASM-06 | COLA timing. The statutory COLA is granted on July 1 of the second calendar year after retirement (ACFR2025, Note 2). The model applies the first COLA at the start of annuity year 3 and compounds annually thereafter, at SRC-05 rates. Va. Code § 51.1-166 D 4 delays commencement for a retiree with fewer than 20 years of creditable service until one full calendar year after the date the member would otherwise have been eligible for an unreduced allowance, and the bill freezes that date without regard to bridge credit; the model applies that delay where a profile has fewer than 20 years. |
| ASM-07 | Salary growth for AFC projection. 3.50% per annum, equal to VRS's published wage inflation assumption (SRC-03). This is the across-the-board component only; VRS adds a service-based merit and longevity component which is not reproduced in the published appendix in machine-readable form. For members with 20+ years of service the merit component is small. Direction of error: understates AFC growth, which understates the forgone accrual in the work-to-eligibility baseline and therefore OVERSTATES the measured subsidy on that baseline. Sensitivity: 2.50% and 4.50%. |
| ASM-08 | AFC construction. AFC at a given age equals the average of the SRC-11 number of months of highest consecutive creditable compensation ending at that age, computed from the stated current salary growing at ASM-07. |
| ASM-09 | Plan 2 / Hybrid 60-5 early-retirement reduction factor. VRS's numeric 60/5 factor table is not published in machine-readable form (SRC-12). Where the model needs a Plan 2 reduced-benefit comparator at age 60 or above, it applies the published Plan 1 55/5 construction (0.5% per month for the first 60 months to unreduced eligibility, 0.4% per month thereafter), measured to the earlier of Social Security NRA and the Rule-of-90 date. Labelled a proxy wherever used. Below age 60 a Plan 2 or Hybrid member cannot retire at all under current law, so no factor is needed and no proxy is used -- which is where the frozen-service baseline for Plan 2 is actually computed. |
| ASM-10 | Plan assignment from published service bands. The published age x service grid (SRC-18) does not identify benefit structure. Benefit structure is inferred from completed total service at June 30, 2025 as a proxy for membership date: Hybrid if service < 11.5 years (membership date on or after January 1, 2014); Plan 2 if 11.5 <= service < 17.5 years (membership date July 1, 2010 - December 31, 2013, plus the January 2008 - June 2010 cohort that lacked 60 months of creditable service at January 1, 2013); Plan 1 if service >= 17.5 years. Members are assumed uniformly distributed within each published band. This proxy FAILS for members with purchased prior service, prior non-contiguous VRS service, part-time service, or a hybrid opt-in; those members carry total service that does not match tenure. The derived split is therefore calibrated to SRC-16 (see ASM-11). This is the largest single source of uncertainty in the population estimate. |
| ASM-11 | Plan split calibration. The derived system-wide Plan 1 / Plan 2 / Hybrid split from ASM-10 is scaled by a single multiplicative factor per benefit structure so that the system total reconciles to SRC-16, with the residual absorbed by Hybrid. Reported in the reconciliation note. |
| ASM-12 | Political subdivision age x service distribution. VRS does not publish an age x service grid for the aggregate political subdivision plan (VAL2025 tabulates state, teacher, SPORS, VaLORS and judicial only). The political subdivision grid is constructed from the state-employee grid shape scaled to the published political subdivision active count (SRC-15), then adjusted by the ratio of published average service (8.3 years for political subdivisions, 10.1 for state) so that the mean of the constructed distribution matches the published political subdivision mean. Direction of error: unquantified. This number should be replaced with VRS data. |
| ASM-13 | Decrements in the population projection. The published grid is rolled forward deterministically (age + 1, service + 1 per year). Members who reach an unreduced threshold are removed at the published retirement rates (SRC-23). All other exits (termination, death, disability) are applied at a flat 3.00% per annum for the 25-to-30-year-service population. Sensitivity: 2.00% and 5.00%. No new entrants are added to Plan 1 or Plan 2 because both benefit structures are closed to new members (Plan 1 closed July 1, 2010; Plan 2 closed January 1, 2014). |
| ASM-14 | Political subdivision member contribution rate taken as the statutory 5% of creditable compensation. VAL2025 does not publish an aggregate political subdivision total normal cost rate, so the political subdivision normal-cost offset is approximated from the published employer normal cost rate plus 5%. |
| ASM-15 | Veteran share of covered membership. SRC-24 gives 6% for the Virginia classified state workforce as of June 30, 2024 (self-identified). No published figure exists for VRS teachers or political subdivision employees. The model uses 6.0% for state employees (published anchor), 2.5% for teachers, and 7.0% for political subdivision non-hazardous employees, with a low/high band of 0.6x / 1.6x on each. Self-identification undercounts, which pushes the central estimate down. |
| ASM-16 | Share of veterans with qualifying active duty service. Va. Code § 51.1-142.2 B requires at least 180 consecutive days of full-time active duty and a discharge that was not dishonorable. Assumed 80% of self-identified veterans satisfy both (low 70%, high 90%). |
| ASM-17 | Share excluded by the military retired pay bar. Va. Code § 51.1-142.2 B bars purchase of service counted in any retirement allowance received or to be received, except as required by 10 U.S.C. ch. 1223 (reserve-component non-regular retired pay). The bill inherits that bar (§ 51.1-142.4 B 4, H 5). Assumed 18% of qualifying veterans are excluded because their service is counted in regular military retired pay (low 12%, high 25%). Federal data: approximately 2.4 million of roughly 18 million U.S. veterans receive military retired pay, but the share is higher among veterans in career government employment. |
| ASM-18 | Share that did not already purchase in the 24-month window. Members who purchased prior active duty service at approximate normal cost within the § 51.1-142.2 B 3 window already hold it as creditable service and gain nothing from the bridge. VRS does not publish purchase-of-prior-service take-up. Assumed 70% did not purchase (low 55%, high 85%). |
| ASM-19 | Behavioural take-up of the bridge among eligible members. Low 10%, Moderate 20%, High 30% (default scenarios where no better data exists). Cross-checked against SRC-23: VRS assumes 12.5% / 7.5% of Plan 1 state employees retire at age 50 once they hold 30 years, and 11.5% of Plan 2 members retire in the year age plus service reaches 90, so a 20% take-up of a purchase that costs several months of salary is not implausible but is not evidence-based. |
| ASM-20 | Amortization of the liability increase into an employer contribution rate. The increase in unfunded accrued liability is amortized as a level percent of payroll over the published amortization period (SRC-15; 20 years for state, teacher and the political subdivision aggregate) at SRC-01 interest and SRC-04 payroll growth. This mirrors VRS's own layered amortization method but is not VRS's calculation. |
| ASM-21 | Replacement-employee cost. Where a workforce effect is shown, the departing member's position is assumed refilled at 100%, 90% and 80% of the departing member's creditable compensation, with employer retirement contributions at the published employer DB rate for the plan. No dollar value is assigned to recruiting, onboarding, vacancy duration, productivity ramp or loss of institutional knowledge. |
| ASM-22 | Behavioural mix. Share of purchasers who, under current law, would have worked on to first unreduced eligibility and then retired (baseline WTE); the remainder would have separated or retired at the purchase date anyway (baseline SUB). Central 0.50, low 0.25, high 0.75. This is the single most consequential assumption in the model and no published data bears on it. |
| ASM-23 | Tenure offset. Benefit structure is set by membership date, but the published grid tabulates total service. The model sets tenure = total service + D with D fitted so that the derived system-wide plan split reproduces the published split (SRC-16). The fitted value is D = +3.0 years with zero dispersion needed, consistent with part-time service, breaks in service, leave, and refunded service not repurchased. |
| ASM-24 | Pre-2013 service share for a Plan 2 cell, used to blend the 1.70% and 1.65% multipliers: 2.5 years (midpoint of the 0-to-5 year range implied by Plan 2 membership dates). |
