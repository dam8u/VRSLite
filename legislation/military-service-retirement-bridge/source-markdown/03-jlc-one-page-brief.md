# MILITARY SERVICE RETIREMENT BRIDGE
### One-page brief for the Joint Leadership Council of Veterans Service Organizations

**Proposal:** Let a vested VRS Plan 1 or Plan 2 member use up to four years of prior active duty military service, at the same low "approximate normal cost" new hires pay, **but only to reach the finish line for an unreduced retirement** — not to raise the pension check.

---

### The gap this fills

A veteran hired into Virginia service today has **24 months** to buy up to four years of active duty military service at approximate normal cost (12.50% of salary per year for Plan 1, 10.74% for Plan 2). After that window closes, the same four years can still be bought — but at **actuarial equivalent cost**, which for a mid-career or late-career employee is several times higher and routinely runs into tens of thousands of dollars.

The practical result: the benefit is priced for people who do not yet know they need it. A 26-year-old new hire rarely has the cash or the reason to buy service credit in year one. The 52-year-old with 27 years in — who now sits three years short of an unreduced retirement and knows exactly what those three years are worth — is the one who needs it, and that is precisely the person current law prices out.

**Nothing in current law bridges that gap.** The 24-month window is a pricing cliff with no second door.

---

### What the proposal does

| | Existing right (unchanged) | New Military Service Retirement Bridge |
|---|---|---|
| When | First 24 months after hire | Any time after, once vested (5 years) |
| Price | Approximate normal cost | **Same approximate normal cost** |
| What you get | Full service credit — counts for everything | **Retirement eligibility only** |
| Raises your monthly pension? | Yes | **No** |

- **Plan 1:** bridge years count toward the 30-year requirement for unreduced retirement at age 50 or later.
- **Plan 2:** bridge years count one-for-one toward the Rule of 90. *Example: age 58 + 28 years of service + 4 bridge years = 90.*
- Capped at the **amount needed to reach the threshold** — never more. Once you qualify, you cannot buy more.
- Stays inside the existing **four-year lifetime military cap**.
- Never makes you older on paper, and never adds a dollar to the benefit formula.
- The existing full-value purchase at actuarial cost **stays available** for anyone who wants military service to count for everything.

### What it deliberately does not touch

Vesting · the benefit formula · PLOP · the health insurance credit · disability retirement · death-in-service benefits · group life · hazardous-duty benefits · COLA start date · portability. The draft bill carries express exclusions for each, plus four conforming amendments that close leaks the concept paper did not catch.

### Core principle

**This is an eligibility benefit, not a pension-accrual benefit.** It lets a veteran who has already given Virginia a full career cross the finish line a little sooner. It does not treat military service as if it were Virginia employment.

---

### What JLCVSO needs to know before it carries this

1. **It costs money, and we should say so.** Approximate normal cost is the price of a year of *accrual*. The bridge sells something else — removal of the early-retirement reduction. VRS's actuaries will almost certainly find the price does not cover the value. Present this as a **deliberate, quantified subsidy for veterans**, not a cost-neutral fix. Every VRS bill gets a mandatory Board impact statement (§ 30-19.1:7); if we claim cost-neutrality and the Board says otherwise, the bill dies.
2. **Most career military retirees may be shut out.** Current law bars purchasing service already counted in another retirement allowance — including military retired pay. Reserve and Guard service is protected by federal law. JLCVSO must decide whether to accept that bar or fight it.
3. **Hybrid plan members are excluded as drafted.** That is nearly everyone hired on or after January 1, 2014 — and the share grows every year. This is the biggest open choice in the package.
4. **PLOP and the bridge are practically either/or** for a member who leaves at the accelerated date. That is the price of keeping PLOP untouched.
