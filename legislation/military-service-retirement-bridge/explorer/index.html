This tool is an illustrative policy-analysis model. It is not an actuarial certification, the VRS Board impact statement required under § 30-19.1:7, or a General Assembly fiscal impact statement. It is not a savings calculator and does not advocate a result. Every figure is tagged as a ●Published Fact, a ◑Derived Estimate, or a ○Scenario Assumption. Prepared by Policy Development Team using legal, policy, and financial analysis tools, pending review by veterans’ legal counsel.
