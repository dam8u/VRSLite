# VRS Statutory Research

## Core rule
Research the law as it exists now. Do not silently blend current law with proposed policy.

## Source hierarchy
Use sources in this order when available:
1. Virginia Code, especially Title 51.1.
2. Official VRS regulations, handbooks, employer manuals, board materials, actuarial publications, and forms.
3. Virginia General Assembly, Division of Legislative Services, JLARC, DPB, House Appropriations, and Senate Finance & Appropriations materials.
4. Federal statutes or guidance when federal military-service protections may apply.
5. Secondary sources only when primary sources do not answer the question.

Prefer the most current version of each source. Record the source date or effective date when it matters.

## Required terminology discipline
Never assume these terms are interchangeable:
- service credit
- creditable service
- earned service
- active service
- benefit-calculation service
- purchased prior service
- granted service
- eligibility-only credit

Before relying on a term, determine how the controlling source uses it and what benefits or calculations it affects.

## Research workflow
1. Identify whether the question concerns Plan 1, Plan 2, or both.
2. Identify the exact benefit or statutory function at issue.
3. Locate the controlling statute or official VRS rule.
4. Check for definitions and cross-references that change the meaning.
5. Check whether purchased military service is treated differently from actual employment service.
6. Check whether timing of purchase changes cost, treatment, or both.
7. Check for Plan 1 and Plan 2 differences.
8. Check for downstream effects on vesting, retirement eligibility, benefit calculation, PLOP, HIC, disability, survivor benefits, group life, portability, refunds, and hazardous-duty provisions when relevant.
9. State any ambiguity or unresolved issue rather than guessing.

## Output standard
For each issue provide:
- Current rule.
- Controlling authority.
- Practical effect.
- Plan 1 / Plan 2 distinction if any.
- Any legal ambiguity or drafting concern.

When comparing current law with a proposal, clearly label:
- CURRENT LAW
- PROPOSED CHANGE
- LEGAL EFFECT

## Accuracy safeguards
Do not infer a benefit consequence merely because a source uses the phrase "service credit." Trace the term to the specific benefit provision.
Do not state that a purchased year equals a worked year for all purposes unless the governing sources expressly support that conclusion.
Do not treat PLOP active-service requirements as satisfied by purchased prior service unless current VRS authority expressly permits it.
